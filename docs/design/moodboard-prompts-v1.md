# Study Planner Pro — Asset Prompt Pack v1

Use these prompts as art direction, not as one-off image generation prompts. The same lens, lighting and materials should be preserved across all scenes.

## Global art direction

Photorealistic editorial still-life for a premium study productivity product, quiet modern library desk, warm off-white paper, graphite, natural wood, understated laptop, soft directional morning light, realistic materials, subtle film grain, restrained depth of field, large negative space, sophisticated art direction, minimal composition, believable physical shadows, no people, no text, no logos, no neon, no holograms, no floating objects, no exaggerated 3D, no fantasy, no glossy plastic, no clutter.

## Dashboard hero

A calm study desk with an open paper planner, a single violet bookmark tab, slim laptop edge, pencil, one ceramic cup in the far background, morning window light from the left, generous empty space on the right for UI overlay, premium editorial photography, 16:9 composition.

## Planner scene

Top-down editorial photograph of a neat paper calendar and weekly study notes on a warm stone desk, one iris-colored tab marking the current day, graphite pencil, subtle page texture, realistic hand-made marks but no readable text, generous negative space, 4:3 composition.

## Focus scene

Close editorial still-life of a minimal desk lamp, notebook, mechanical pencil and a small analog timer-like object, soft evening light, restrained warm shadows, deep calm atmosphere, no glowing digital effects, premium product photography, 16:9.

## Subjects scene

A carefully arranged set of three study materials — textbook, notebook and flashcards — organized on a neutral desk, distinct but muted accent tabs identifying categories, natural daylight, sophisticated catalog/editorial photography, clean geometry, 4:3.

## Empty state / onboarding

Single open notebook on a quiet desk with one bookmark and a pencil, slightly angled perspective, soft morning light, large negative space, emotionally calm, optimistic but not motivational, premium editorial visual, 4:3.

## Negative prompt

cartoon, anime, illustration unless explicitly requested, student stock photo, smiling person at laptop, fake productivity desk, overly perfect staged room, colorful clutter, neon purple glow, holographic UI, floating books, 3D blob, surreal geometry, low resolution, oversharpened, plastic materials, excessive bokeh, text, watermark, logo.
